Injury-risk analysis

Spot injury risk before it costs games

PlayerDNA reads movement quality, symmetry and load to flag the warning signs of injury early — so they can be reviewed before they become a problem. These are indicators that prompt professional review, never a medical diagnosis.

What it screens

Early signals, from movement

Left–right asymmetry

Single-leg hop-test LSI and movement screens highlight imbalances between sides.

Fatigue & overload

Repeated-effort and load markers flag when an athlete is being pushed too hard.

Movement quality

FMS, Y-Balance and posture screening assess stability and control.

2D video kinematics

Joint angles measured frame by frame reveal mechanics that raise risk.

Return-to-play readiness

A conservative readiness view (hop-test LSI ≥ 90%) — always paired with professional sign-off.

Graded & routed

Each risk is graded and routed to professional review when it's elevated.

Important: PlayerDNA provides sport-performance screening. Risk outputs are indicators that prompt professional review — they are not a medical diagnosis and do not replace a doctor, physiotherapist or qualified clinician.
